Meet the Author Behind JEMM

Hi there! I'm Cesar Bianchi, the developer and maintainer of JEMM – Jellyfin Easy Metadata Manager. I am a technology enthusiast, software developer, and strong advocate of open-source software. Throughout my professional and personal journey, I have always been passionate about building practical solutions that simplify complex processes and improve the user experience.

One of my greatest passions is Jellyfin and JEMM is a labor of love, built with open-source spirit and a lot of coffee ☕. I’m always excited to hear from fellow developers, contributors, and users who want to improve the project, share ideas, or just say hello. This has allowed me to develop a deep understanding of Jellyfin's architecture, metadata management, library organization, automation capabilities, integrations, troubleshooting techniques, and best practices for maintaining large media collections.

My extensive hands-on experience with Jellyfin was one of the primary motivations behind creating Jellyfin Easy Metadata Manager. I wanted to build a solution that would help users manage their media metadata more efficiently while maintaining the flexibility and control that make Jellyfin such an outstanding open-source media platform.

I am also available to collaborate on professional Jellyfin-related projects. Whether you need consulting services, architecture guidance, deployment planning, metadata strategy, workflow automation, system integration, performance optimization, or custom development, I bring extensive real-world experience and a practical problem-solving mindset to every project.

For me, Jellyfin is much more than just a media server. It is a platform that empowers users to take full ownership of their digital media experience. I am proud to contribute to that ecosystem and remain committed to creating tools and solutions that help the Jellyfin community thrive.

Feel free to reach out to me at cesar_bianchi@hotmail.com — whether you want to contribute, ask questions, or support the project in any way.

Thanks for being part of the JEMM journey! 💙
